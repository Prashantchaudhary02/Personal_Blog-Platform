About the Author & Platform

Prashant Chaudhary

Staff Technologist, Writer & Open Source Craftsman

Welcome to Chronicle — a calm, minimalist personal blog platform built to publish ideas without the algorithmic noise of modern social networks.

Every post is composed in clean Markdown, stored locally in your browser, and rendered with custom typographic scales optimized for deep reading.

Key Platform Features

  • Pure Markdown Writing: Distraction-free composer with live formatting toolbar and real-time preview.
  • Dark & Light Modes: Fluid theme transitions crafted with accessible color palettes.
  • Full Reader View: Immersive reading mode with scroll progress indicator, claps, and comments.
  • Client-Side Privacy: 100% private, runs offline, data persists in your browser with JSON export/import.
